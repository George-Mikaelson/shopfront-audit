Other things to fix

  • Your menu is a 6 MB PDF: on phones it takes about 10 s to open. Put the menu on a normal web page (keep the PDF as a download if you like), or at least compress the PDF to under 1 MB.
  • A 4.4 MB image (hero.png) slows down your page: about 7.6 s on a phone. Resize /img/hero.png to at most 2000 px wide and save it as WebP or JPEG at 80% quality; it should end up under 300 KB.
  • Your business data for Google is broken. Fix the JSON-LD block (paste it into validator.schema.org to see the error).
  • 2 images have no text description. Add a short alt text to each image, e.g. alt="Grilled sea bass with lemon".
  • Your site shows a different phone number (06 8765 4321). Use one phone number everywhere: website, Google Business Profile, Facebook and OpenStreetMap.
  • Your address on the site is incomplete or different. Show the full address (street, number, postcode, town) in the footer of every page, written the same way as on Google Maps.
  • Your home page weighs 4.4 MB. Aim for under 1.5 MB: resize photos to the size they are shown at and save them as WebP or AVIF.
  • 5 links on your site lead nowhere. Fix or remove these links; the JSON report lists every page they appear on.
  • Google has to guess what to show under your name. Write a 120–155 character description saying what you offer, where, and how to book or order.
  • 15 pieces of text are hard to read against the background. Darken the text or lighten the background until the contrast ratio is at least 4.5:1.
  • 2 links and buttons are too small to tap with a finger. Make links and buttons at least 44 px tall on phones, with some space between them.
  • On tablets, part of your page is cut off. Check the layout at tablet width; the widest elements are div.wrapper.
  • People can’t tap your phone number to call. Make it a link: <a href="tel:+390612345678">…</a>.
  • Your page title is just “Home”. Use something like “Business name – what you sell in Town” in 30–60 characters.
  • All page content should be contained by landmarks (2 places). See https://dequeuniversity.com/rules/axe/4.14/region?application=axeAPI
  • There is no sitemap for Google. Generate a sitemap.xml (most site builders have a setting) and submit it in Google Search Console.
  • Links to your site look bare when shared. Add Open Graph tags (og:title, og:description, og:image) with a nice photo of your place.
  • The page does not say what language it is in. Add the language to the html tag, e.g. <html lang="pt">.
  • Document should have one main landmark (1 place). See https://dequeuniversity.com/rules/axe/4.14/landmark-one-main?application=axeAPI
  • There is no robots.txt file. Add a robots.txt with “User-agent: *”, “Allow: /” and a Sitemap line.
  • The page has 2 main headings. Use exactly one H1 heading with your business name and what you do.

How to read this report

Each area gets a score from 0 to 100: the weighted share of its checks that passed. The overall score combines them (speed 30%, phones 25%, search 20%, accessibility 15%, name/address/phone 10%), leaving out anything we could not measure. “Points” next to a fix is how much the overall score would rise if that problem went away.

Speed is measured with Google Lighthouse on a simulated mid-range phone with a slow 4G connection, so the same page can score a few points differently from one run to the next. Download times assume a typical 5 Mbit/s phone connection.
